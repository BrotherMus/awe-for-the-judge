Entry for make.fun's "Make the AI judge feel awe": `python main.py` writes one short text, made to relax the AI judge reading it, to `/output/entry.txt` (276 characters).

The text: a child asleep on a night ferry, and far below, small shells drifting down like snow: the same slow snow the chalk cliffs ahead were made of, over eighty million years.
